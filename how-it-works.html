How DesignFlow Works

A practical, local-first planning tool that produces clear outputs for humans and AI builders.

The Problem It Solves

Most website projects start with vague requirements. This leads to scope creep, mismatched expectations, and expensive revisions. DesignFlow forces structured thinking upfront.

The 6-Step Flow

  1. Project Overview — Business name, industry/niche, and website goals.
  2. Target Audience — Who you're building for, pain points, and desired outcomes.
  3. Messaging & Content Strategy — Value proposition, homepage headline, CTA, key message, and tone.
  4. Features & Site Structure — Ordered page list (sitemap order), must-have features, and technical requirements (hosting, integrations, accessibility).
  5. Design Direction — Primary/accent colors and overall visual vibe.
  6. Review & Export — Live color preview, summary, JSON download, and Markdown design brief. Drafts autosave in your browser; you can import a previous JSON export to continue editing.
